How do I play?

Every chapter of a story is a cryptogram: each letter has been swapped for another letter. The small letter under each box is the code. Tap a box and type the real letter you think it is, and every box with the same code fills in. Solve a chapter to read it and unlock the next one. "How to play" in the app explains it again.

What is free, and what does the unlock include?

Five stories are free. One in-app purchase unlocks all the other stories, including stories added in future updates. It is a one-time payment: no subscription and no ads.

I bought the unlock, but the stories are locked on my new phone

iPhone: tap a locked story, then Restore purchase. Use the same Apple ID that made the purchase. If Family Sharing is on, family members get the unlock too.

Android: sign in to Google Play with the same Google account that made the purchase, then open the app. The unlock comes back on its own, or tap Restore purchase.

How do I get a refund?

Apple and Google handle the purchases. On iPhone, ask for a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com. On Android, use Google Play's refund page.

I think a fact in a story is wrong

We write every story from well documented sources, but please tell us. Email the story name and chapter number and we will check it.
