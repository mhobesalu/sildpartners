How do I play?

Every case has a few suspects. Each suspect matches exactly one item in every column, and no two suspects share one. Read the clues and mark the grid: one tap for ✕ ("definitely not"), two taps for ✓ ("this is it"). When you know who did it, fill in the case notes, answer the question and press Check. "How to play" in Settings explains it again.

What is free, and what does the unlock include?

Five stories are free at every difficulty, and so is Today's case. One in-app purchase unlocks all the other stories, including stories added in future updates. It is a one-time payment: no subscription and no ads.

I bought the unlock, but the stories are locked on my new phone

iPhone: open Settings in the app, tap Unlock all, then Restore purchase. Use the same Apple ID that made the purchase. If Family Sharing is on, family members get the unlock too.

Android: sign in to Google Play with the same Google account that made the purchase, then open the app. The unlock comes back on its own, or tap Restore purchase.

How do I get a refund?

Apple and Google handle the purchases. On iPhone, ask for a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com. On Android, use Google Play's refund page.

Is a puzzle broken?

Every case is generated with exactly one solution. If you think one is wrong, send us the case number shown under the story title (for example "Case #1A2B3C") and we will look at it.
