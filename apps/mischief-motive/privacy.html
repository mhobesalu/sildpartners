Privacy policy

Mischief & Motive · Last updated 19 November 2026 (Android added)

In short: the app does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Who we are

Mischief & Motive is published by Sild Growth OÜ (registry code 14067479), Telliskivi 33-1, 10611 Tallinn, Estonia. Contact: maria@sildpartners.com.

What stays on your device

Your current puzzle, your settings and your solved-case counts are saved only on your own device, so you can pick up where you left off. We cannot see them. Deleting the app deletes them.

Purchases

The one-time unlock is sold through Apple's App Store on iPhone and Google Play on Android. Apple or Google handles the payment and your payment details. We do not receive your name, email or card details. The app only asks the store whether your account has bought the unlock. See Apple's privacy policy and Google's privacy policy.

Ratings and reviews

The app may occasionally show the store's standard "rate this app" prompt. Apple or Google runs that prompt, and we do not receive any personal data from it.

Children

The app is made for adults and older teenagers. It does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

Email

If you email us, we use your email address and message only to answer you, and delete them when they are no longer needed.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask what data we hold about you and ask us to delete it. Because the app collects nothing, the only data we could hold is an email you sent us. You can also complain to the Estonian Data Protection Inspectorate (aki.ee).

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
