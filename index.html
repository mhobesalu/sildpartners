Sild Partners Impact Studio · Tallinn, Estonia

Building bridges where business meets meaning.

We help festivals, conferences and causes in Estonia, the Baltics and the Nordics find sponsors, and help companies there give back in a way that fits their brand.

We're a sponsorship sales partner, not an event agency: we don't organise your event, we find the sponsors for it, for a fixed fee plus a success commission. A good fit if you run a festival, expo, tech or women-in-leadership event, or a cause with a real audience, and want sponsors for your next edition 3 to 9 months from now. Also a fit if your company has 50 to 300 people and wants one clear giving-back programme instead of scattered donations. Not a fit if you need money in under 6 weeks, want a PR or ad agency, or are raising funds for a private person.

03 — How it works

You talk.
We do the rest.

  1. 01

    Brief

    Week 1. A 30-minute call about who you want to meet, in which countries, and what you can offer them.

  2. 02

    Target list

    Week 1. We build a researched list of organisations and named contacts. You approve it before anything is sent.

  3. 03

    Outreach

    Weeks 2 to 4. Personal messages in English or Estonian, with follow-ups. Every reply is handled within a day.

  4. 04

    Meetings

    Usually weeks 3 to 8. Busy decision-makers take time to answer, so we keep following up after the outreach weeks. Interested contacts land in your calendar with a short note on who they are and what they want.

Pricing: for events and causes, a fixed fee to design your sponsor package, and a one-off fee plus a share of the sponsorship we actually bring in. For companies, a fixed-price giving-back sprint. For export, a 4-week Pilot Sprint where part of the fee is paid only once your first meeting happens.

06 — Questions

Straight
answers.

Are you an event agency?

No. We don't organise events. We do the sponsorship sales part: research, outreach to decision-makers and meetings, like a freelance sponsorship manager or business developer on a 2 to 4 month project, paid a fixed fee plus a success fee. Many event agencies organise well but don't sell sponsorship.

Who do you work with?

Festivals, expos, conferences and causes looking for sponsors, and companies with 50 to 300 people that want to give back with meaning. Mostly in Estonia, the Baltics and the Nordics, with outreach to 20+ countries.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the size of your event or company and what you need, so every client gets its own offer. The fee is fixed and agreed upfront, so there are no surprises. For sponsor outreach we add a share of the sponsorship money that actually comes in, so we earn more only when you do. You get a written offer after a free 20-minute intro call.

How early should we start looking for sponsors?

About 4 months before the event. Many companies set sponsorship budgets between December and February, and busy decision-makers often take 3 to 8 weeks to say yes to a meeting.

Do you guarantee sponsors?

No one honestly can. We guarantee the work: a researched target list you approve, personal outreach, follow-ups and every interested contact passed to you. You take the calls and sign the deals.

Who sends the emails?

We do, as Sild Partners on your behalf, in English or Estonian.

When are we not a good fit?

If you need money within 6 weeks, want paid ads or PR, or have no audience or story a sponsor can stand behind yet. We'll say so on the first call.
