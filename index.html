How it works

  1. Brief

    A 30-minute call about who you want to meet, in which countries, and what you can offer them.

  2. Target list

    We build a researched list of organisations and named contacts. You approve it before anything is sent.

  3. Outreach

    Personal messages in English or Estonian, with follow-ups. Every reply is handled within a day.

  4. Meetings

    Interested contacts land in your calendar with a short note on who they are and what they want.

Pricing: a monthly retainer plus a fee per qualified meeting, so you pay mostly for results.
